BOUNCER - FIRST PIN
===================
Write the household's first 4-digit PIN after "PIN=" below, save this file,
then put the SD card into the Raspberry Pi. Bouncer reads the PIN once at its
first start and then removes it from this file. The household must replace it
with their own PIN at their first login.

DE: Tragen Sie die erste 4-stellige PIN hinter "PIN=" ein und speichern Sie.
FR: Inscrivez le premier code PIN à 4 chiffres après "PIN=" et enregistrez.
